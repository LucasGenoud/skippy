# Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy covers the Skippy apps and the public instance at
<https://skippy-notes.com>, operated by Lucas Genoud. A Skippy server run by
someone else is governed by that operator's policy, not this one.

## What is stored

When you use the public instance, the server stores:

- Your account: name, email address, and a hash of your password. The
  password itself is never stored.
- What you write: notes, checklists, labels, workspaces, collections, and
  version history.
- Files you attach: images, audio recordings, and other files, plus text
  recognized in images so they can be searched.
- Your settings, including places you save for location reminders. Saved
  places are personal: people you share notes with never see them.

## What stays on your device

The app keeps a copy of your notes on the device so it works offline. Your
position is read only when you save a place, and arriving at or leaving a
saved place is detected on the device. Apart from the places you save, your
position is never sent to the server.

The microphone records only while you record an audio note. The camera and
photo library are used only when you attach or save an image.

## What is not collected

Skippy has no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Nothing is sold or
shared with data brokers.

## Services you choose to connect

Some features send data to a third party, and only when you set them up:

- AI features send the relevant note text to the AI provider the workspace
  owner configured.
- Reminder notifications go through the channel you configure, such as
  ntfy, Telegram, or email.
- Link previews are fetched by the server from the linked website.
- The map used to pick a place loads tiles from OpenStreetMap, which sees
  your IP address.

## Sharing

Notes are visible to the members of the workspace that holds them and to
people you share a note with. No one else can read them.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a note moves it to the trash, and emptying the trash removes it.
To delete your account, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account.
This deletes your account and the workspaces you own, with their notes and
files. Notes you wrote in someone else's workspace stay there, without your
name.
